Mallorca in October is when the island eases into a quieter, gentler rhythm. Fiery crimson sunsets across the bay in Camp de Mar, a rainbow arching into the Mediterranean beneath dark rain clouds, the rugged cliffs of Serra de Tramuntana crowned by a solitary pine, and the sublime play of light through the rose window of La Seu cathedral in Palma.
